Every budgeting app wants read access to my bank account, and most of them want
a subscription for the privilege. This one runs on my own machine, reads exports
I give it, and tells nobody anything.

## What it does

It answers one question honestly: where did the money actually go. Not a
projection, not a category pie chart that lumps half the year into "other", just
the real movements grouped the way I actually think about them.

## Why it stays private

There is no hosted version and no sign-up, which is the point. It is built for
one very specific user, and that user already has the data.
