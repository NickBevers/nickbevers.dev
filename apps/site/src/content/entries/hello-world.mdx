This entry is scaffolding. It is here so the entry template has real content to
lay out while the site is being built, and it will be deleted before anything
real is published. Every element below is something the design calls for, so if
one of them looks wrong here, it will look wrong on a real post too.

The paragraph above gets a drop cap. It is drawn with `::first-letter`, which
means the text stays whole in the DOM and a screen reader reads the word
normally rather than spelling out the first letter.

## A heading, to check the rhythm

Section headings sit at 27px with tight tracking, and the rule beneath them is
2px solid ink. The prose measure caps at 680px regardless of how wide the window
gets, because a line of text that runs the full width of a desktop monitor is
tiring to read.

Body copy runs at 18px with generous leading. Links inside prose, [like this
one](https://github.com/NickBevers), take a dotted underline rather than a solid
one, and wash to the accent background on hover.
